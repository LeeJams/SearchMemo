# Service icon sources

Retrieved 2026-10-15. Brand marks belong to their respective owners.

- Grok: https://grok.com/images/favicon.svg — background and original white mark paths, without browser-only filters.
- Microsoft Copilot: https://copilot.microsoft.com/static/cmc/favicon.svg — original paths and gradients adapted to react-native-svg.
- DeepSeek: https://www.deepseek.com/favicon.ico — largest image converted losslessly to PNG, transparency preserved.

Grok and Copilot paths live in components/icons/SearchIcons.js. These icons are bundled locally and require no network request at runtime.
